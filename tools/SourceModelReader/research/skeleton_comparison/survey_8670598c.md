## Interpretation limits

- Only Unit, TransformInfo and MeshInfo tables on the TOC side are read; geometry, skin attributes and BoneInfo palette validation are not evaluated here.
- A non-identity direct binding is a candidate discriminator, not proof: the mesh may be unskinned auxiliary geometry, which the full Unit JSON decode has to confirm.
- LOD values are taken from MeshInfo records as stored, including negative entries.
- Surveying one package says nothing about packages that were not read.
